## STATO

Credit Alejandra Gonzalez-Beltran, Orlaith Burke, Philippe Rocca-Serra and
the STATO contributors. The STATO 1.5 snapshot at revision
a5910e6115217a88ee86e2666e12983ef2ef2a42 declares
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
[Original source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ISA-tools/stato/a5910e6115217a88ee86e2666e12983ef2ef2a42/releases/1.5/stato.owl)

The [native admission ledger](reviews/approved/batches/stato.json) lists
each published STATO identifier, its canonical IRI and the exact modules
containing the selected labels and SKOS subject scope. The
[relation ledger](reviews/relations/stato-decisions.json) identifies the
reviewed native subject and object pairs and projection rationale.
Curricula converts selected native labels and subject organization into
SKOS concepts and editorial taxonomy, and projects reviewed axiom roles
into scientific relations with provenance. Embedded OBI-owned material
retains its separate attribution and terms. OBI predicate identifiers
appear in source witnesses, while OBI-owned classes are excluded as native
admission and projection endpoints. Downstream conditions remain subject
to owner or qualified rights review. [OBI project](https://obi-ontology.org/)

## OpenDSA

The [pinned repository README](https://github.com/OpenDSA/OpenDSA/blob/7f7d2baa355e0b649982d1c48d17705185bfc2ea/README.md)
states the MIT notice. Reviewed reStructuredText chapters also carry
file-level OpenDSA contributor copyright and MIT headers. The PDA
frames identify their authors and institution. The linked JFLAP example
carries no separate licence notice in its bytes. Curricula distributes
references, file and passage fingerprints, and its own scientific judgments.
The underlying textbook text, code and examples stay outside the public
RDF package. Owner confirmation of the evidence-only scope remains open.
